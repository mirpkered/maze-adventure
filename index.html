A QUICK GUIDE

How to play

Trace the open path

  1. Touch or click the START entrance.
  2. Drag through the open corridors to the flower.
  3. Walls stop the pencil. To leave a dead end, trace back along your line.
  4. Lift your finger whenever you need to. Touch the end of your line to continue.

Keyboard option: focus the maze and use the arrow keys to move through one passage at a time. There is no countdown and no penalty for taking your time.
